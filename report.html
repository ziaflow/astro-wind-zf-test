Technical Health & Cannibalization

Internal Keyword Cannibalization on Brand

  • Problem: Search Console records 12 distinct URLs indexing for the brand query "ziaflow", including non-canonical HTTP URLs (http://ziaflow.com/), service pages, and legal pages.
  • Change: Enforce a single canonical authority for brand search by pointing all internal brand logos to https://ziaflow.com and ensuring strict HTTP-to-HTTPS 301 redirects at the edge.
  • Expected effect: Concentrates search equity onto the homepage, eliminating split impressions and raising homepage CTR above 5%.

Service Architecture Disconnect

  • Problem: The hub page https://ziaflow.com/services generated only 1 impression (position 68), while individual sub-services receive up to 46 impressions.
  • Change: Rework /services into an index linking directly to Phoenix sub-services (SEO, Web Development, Automation, PPC) with clear internal anchor text.
  • Expected effect: Distributes page authority evenly, helping sub-service pages break through page 1 (positions 1–3) for localized commercial searches.

What to do next

  1. Submit https://ziaflow.com/sitemap-index.xml directly in Search Console (the legacy /sitemap.xml URL returns a 404).
  2. Consolidate service page internal linking from the header, footer, and /services hub to resolve internal keyword competition.
  3. Optimize on-page metadata for crm automation for small business arizona and lead follow up automation on /services/automation to lift rankings from page 2 into page 1.
