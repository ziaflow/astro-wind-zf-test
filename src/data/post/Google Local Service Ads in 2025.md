## Step-by-Step LSA Setup for 2026

1. **Compliance Fundamentals:** Gather licenses, insurance, and ensure [Google Business Profile (GBP)](/services/gmb-optimization) accuracy.
2. **Google Screening & Verification:** Upload required documents and complete background checks.
3. **Profile Optimization:** Add professional photos, staff bios, detailed service menus, FAQs, and accurate contact info.
4. **Review Push:** Encourage and request reviews from satisfied clients via [automated review workflows](/services/review-management).
5. **Launch & Monitor:** Link GBP, set local service areas, and connect instant lead routing so every call or message is answered immediately.

## FAQs About LSAs in 2026

- **Cost:** Usually $25–$85 per lead, varying by industry and metro competitiveness.
- **Approval Time:** 2–5 business days generally; 1–2 weeks for law and medical sectors.
- **Badge Denial Causes:** Incomplete documents, mismatched business names on insurance certificates, or insufficient reviews.
- **Reviews Needed:** Minimum 3–10 depending on industry, ideally growing consistently every month.
- **Multi-Location Management:** Centralized dashboards support unified lead and review tracking.
