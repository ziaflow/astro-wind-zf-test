## The Operational Bottleneck

Commercial HVAC emergencies cannot wait 12 hours for a callback. Desert Peak Mechanical was receiving 30+ high-value service and installation inquiries per week across the Phoenix metropolitan area, but qualified requests were stalled in a generic email inbox:

- Inbound requests lacked equipment serial numbers and building square footage.
- Staff spent 45 minutes manually triaging each lead before scheduling an estimator.
- Competitors with immediate automated scheduling were winning critical bid opportunities.

## The Solution

ZiaFlow engineered an end-to-end diagnostic intake pipeline built on modern serverless infrastructure:

1. **Structured Inbound Diagnostics**: Custom progressive forms capturing commercial tonnage, facility type, and service urgency without client dropoff.
2. **Edge Validation & Routing**: Edge functions scrubbing spam submissions and calculating instant dispatch priority.
3. **CRM & SMS Dispatch Sync**: On-call technicians receive instant SMS alerts with customer history and location coordinates.

## Measured Outcomes

- **42% faster lead-to-quote turnaround**: Reduced initial diagnostic response from 3.8 hours to 18 minutes.
- **Zero dropped leads**: Full visibility from initial web click to field service invoice.
- **$180,000 in recovered pipeline** within the first 60 days of deployment.
