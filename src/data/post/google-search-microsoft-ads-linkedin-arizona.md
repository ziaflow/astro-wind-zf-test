The question is not which platform always produces the cheapest lead. The question is where the intended customer is making a relevant decision, and whether your business can serve the response.

A search for a local repair and a B2B buyer evaluating a longer project can require different offers and follow-up. Platform names alone do not settle the choice.

## Compare the buying situation

Google Search and Microsoft Advertising can be evaluated around expressed search intent. LinkedIn planning begins with a specific business audience and a reason for that audience to respond. Actual targeting capabilities, account settings, and policies need review before launch.

Google Local Services Ads is a separate product from ordinary Search campaigns. Before including it in a contractor or professional-service plan, check current eligibility and account requirements for the category and territory. Do not assume that every business or location qualifies.

## Use one offer and one business definition

Choose a service, a territory, and the action the visitor should take. Define the difference between an ad click, a form attempt, an accepted inquiry, a qualified request, and a booked conversation.

| Decision    | What to document                                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Audience    | Who should respond, and who should not            |
| Offer       | What the visitor receives or can request          |
| Destination | The page or approved lead form used in the test   |
| Budget      | The media boundary and review point               |
| Follow-up   | The owner, expected response, and stop conditions |
| Outcome     | What evidence makes the inquiry qualified         |

## Test the entire handoff before spending

Open the destination on mobile, follow the main action, and test validation and failure handling. Use synthetic QA records so the test does not inflate business reporting. Confirm that campaign parameters are retained where the implementation supports them.

A lower reported conversion cost can be misleading when one account counts contact-page visits and another counts accepted submissions. Align the definitions before comparing platforms.

## Decide what the test taught you

Review actual spend and the quality of responses with the person handling inquiries. A decision to pause can be useful if the offer, audience, or operating capacity does not fit. A decision to expand needs evidence; it should not depend on an industry-wide multiplier presented as your own result.
