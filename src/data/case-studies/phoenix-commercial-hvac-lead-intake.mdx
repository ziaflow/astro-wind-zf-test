## The Operational Bottleneck

Commercial HVAC emergencies cannot wait 12 hours for a callback. Desert Peak Mechanical was receiving 30+ high-value service and installation inquiries per week, but qualified requests were stalled in a generic email inbox:

- Inbound requests lacked equipment serials and building square footage.
- Staff spent 45 minutes manually triaging each lead before scheduling an estimator.
- Competitors with immediate automated scheduling were winning bid opportunities.

## The Solution

ZiaFlow engineered an end-to-end diagnostic intake pipeline built on Astro and Supabase:

1. **Structured Inbound Diagnostics**: Custom Astro progressive form capturing commercial tonnage, facility type, and service urgency without client dropoff.
2. **Edge Validation & Routing**: Cloudflare/Vercel edge functions scrubbing spam and calculating instant dispatch priority.
3. **HubSpot Two-Way Sync**: On-call technicians receive instant SMS alerts with customer history and location coordinates.

## Measured Outcomes

- **42% faster lead-to-quote turnaround**: Reduced initial diagnostic response from 3.8 hours to 18 minutes.
- **Zero dropped leads**: Full visibility from initial web click to field service invoice.
- **$180,000 in recovered pipeline** within the first 60 days of deployment.
