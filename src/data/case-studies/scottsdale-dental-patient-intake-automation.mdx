## The Operational Bottleneck

Patients routinely arrived 15 minutes late and spent another 20 minutes filling out clipboard paper forms in the waiting area. Front-desk staff were backlogged manually retyping handwritten medical histories into their practice management system:

- 18% appointment delay cascading across morning treatment blocks.
- Front desk spent 14 hours per week re-entering patient data and scanning insurance cards.
- High no-show rate on follow-up recare procedures.

## The Solution

ZiaFlow deployed a unified mobile-first check-in and automated patient retention workflow:

1. **Pre-Visit Fast Check-In**: SMS link delivered 24 hours prior allows patients to upload insurance and complete medical history directly on mobile via a secure, zero-friction Astro web app.
2. **Real-Time Verification**: Supabase Edge Functions sanitize and encrypt responses, notifying the front-desk monitor instantly.
3. **Automated Two-Way Recall**: Patient recall reminders synced to practice calendars, keeping chairs filled without receptionist phone tag.

## Measured Outcomes

- **94% completion before arrival**: Average in-lobby wait time reduced from 22 minutes to 3 minutes.
- **14 staff hours saved weekly per location**: Administrative staff shifted from data entry to high-touch patient care.
- **$72,000 annualized recovery** from reclaimed chair time and reduced cancellations.
