## The problem

Running Google Local Service Ads and Google Business Profiles across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, and Chandler without unified attribution meant the operations team could not distinguish qualified service calls from duplicate or out-of-territory inquiries. Post-job review requests were handled manually, causing inconsistent review velocity.

## What changed

ZiaFlow engineered a unified local search and intake workflow connecting Google Business Profiles, LSA lead routing, and automated post-service follow-up.

- Structured service-area landing pages with verified `LocalBusiness` and `Service` JSON-LD schema
- Direct routing of LSA and organic inquiries into a single status pipeline with duplicate suppression
- Automated SMS/email review requests triggered upon job completion to compound local map pack authority

## The outcome

The operations team gained a single source of truth for every inbound call and form submission, cutting manual administrative triage by hours each week while maintaining steady local review growth.
