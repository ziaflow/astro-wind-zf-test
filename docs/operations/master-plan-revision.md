# October 18 revision — authoritative operating roadmap

This revision supersedes the earlier master plan where ownership, timing, content commitments or automation authority conflict. Preserve earlier material as historical context. Dates are forecasts; gates authorize progression. Paperclip is retired.

## Partner-first operating model

Superhuman Docs is the principal internal operating hub and the basis for separate private client hubs. Prioritize genuine use of Superhuman Docs, Superhuman Go and Customer.io partner benefits. Confirm covered features and expiry before relying on them. No new subscriptions or automatic cancellations.

Superhuman Go prepares source-linked briefs, discovery context and update drafts in the flow of work, using approved context and existing permitted connectors. Jeremy reviews these in Docs. Do not enable new permissions or scheduled triggers by default. Do not duplicate Hermes schedules or create another authoritative backlog.

Linear is the authoritative internal work queue, replacing the earlier plan's assignment of that role to ClickUp. GitHub stores code, checks, review and release evidence. Orca manages engineering execution; Hermes prepares recurring operational work only after review of its runtime and stop procedure. Atomic Agent and Antigravity are optional scoped specialists. ClickUp provides existing data or a client view only if the pilot needs it; no competing editable backlog.

Jeremy owns priorities, commitments, exact-version approvals, publishing, releases, pricing and spending. Continue ordinary human-led opportunity follow-up, discovery calls and proposals from day one, while external automation remains disabled.

## Controls

Every issue needs outcome, scope, accountable human, one executing agent, permitted data, dependencies, acceptance criteria, evidence and next action. Agent completion is not human acceptance. Use Backlog → Todo → In Progress → In Review → Done, with approval-needed/blocked labels.

Bind approval to exact action, content/artifact version, recipient or deployment target, and scope. Material changes, expiry and an emergency stop invalidate prior approvals. Recheck execution eligibility immediately before action; unknown provider outcomes require reconciliation before retry.

Emergency stop must separately address local dispatch, active workers, GitHub schedules and Customer.io queued/scheduled actions. A local flag cannot cancel a message already accepted by a remote provider. Restart requires queue reconciliation and Jeremy's approval. Keep normal manual sales and intake available.

Back up operational records and configuration without credentials. Test restoration in isolation and verify completeness. A local pilot-store snapshot is not a backup of vendor-owned databases, documents or files.

## Internal and client versions

Internal: priorities, private communications/notes, effort, costs, partner register, exceptions, approvals and source-linked delivery evidence.

Client: completed work, results/limitations, next steps, decisions, approved milestones, website/marketing/submission/lead/call/booking metrics, linked documents/files, reviewed emails, CSV downloads and verified invoice-status links.

Use separate private documents and folders. Hidden pages/filters are not access controls. Google Drive is the default file location; retain existing Dropbox where appropriate. Show source and reporting period; unavailable data is not zero; phone/booking clicks are not confirmed calls/appointments. No internal notes or other clients' records in client exports.

Weekly client email is prepared automatically but only the exact version approved by Jeremy may be delivered. No synthetic demonstration data goes to real clients.

## Capacity and budget

Service-page improvements and articles are capacity dependent, not mandatory weekly or monthly production commitments. One verified implementation can supply evidence for both. Reuse partner demonstrations only with accurate claims and client permission.

Preserve current partner subscriptions. Verify gross cost, partner credit, actual out-of-pocket cost, metered usage and benefit expiry. Unknown costs are not zero. Target $90 committed and metered monthly spending with a buffer strictly below $100. Surface conflicts before spending; no upgrades or automatic cancellations.
